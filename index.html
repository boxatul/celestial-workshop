About the workshop

Celestial holography

Three weeks at Harvard devoted to celestial holography.

The Simons Collaboration Workshop on Celestial Holography will bring together researchers working on celestial holography, asymptotic symmetries, gravitational scattering, infrared physics, and related aspects of quantum gravity.

The workshop is designed to encourage extended discussion and collaboration alongside a programme of research talks.
